# Dennis Ritchie

1. Dennis MacAlistair Ritchie was an American computer scientist.
2. He developed the C programming language around 1972.
3. C was developed at Bell Laboratories.
4. He made significant contributions to the development of the UNIX operating system.
5. He worked with Ken Thompson and other researchers.
6. His work had a major influence on modern computer programming.
7. C made system programming more efficient and portable.
